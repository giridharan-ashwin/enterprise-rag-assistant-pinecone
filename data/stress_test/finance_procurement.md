# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Finance Procurement

## Purchase Requests
Purchase requests should identify the business purpose, estimated amount, and required delivery date before approval.

## Purchase Approval
Purchases above the department approval limit require procurement review before an order is placed.

## Preferred Vendors
Employees should use approved vendors when a suitable option exists. Exceptions require documented business justification.

## Purchase Orders
A purchase order should be issued before committing to a vendor for purchases that require procurement processing.

## Contract Review
Vendor agreements containing material financial or data-processing commitments require legal and procurement review.
