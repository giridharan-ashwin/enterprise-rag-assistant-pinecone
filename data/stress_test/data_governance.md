# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Data Governance

## Data Classification
Enterprise data should be classified according to sensitivity and business impact. Classification determines handling requirements.

## Data Ownership
Every critical dataset should have a named business owner and a technical steward.

## Data Quality
Critical datasets should define measurable quality rules for completeness, accuracy, timeliness, and consistency.

## Data Access
Access to sensitive data should follow least privilege and be reviewed periodically.

## Data Lineage
Critical analytical datasets should maintain lineage from source systems through major transformation steps.
