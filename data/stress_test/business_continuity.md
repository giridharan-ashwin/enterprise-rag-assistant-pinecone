# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Business Continuity

## Recovery Objectives
Critical systems should define recovery time objectives and recovery point objectives based on business impact.

## Continuity Plans
Business continuity plans should identify critical processes, dependencies, responsible teams, and alternate procedures.

## Disaster Recovery Tests
Critical recovery procedures should be exercised periodically and tracked for corrective actions.

## Emergency Contacts
Continuity plans should maintain current emergency contacts for responsible teams and key suppliers.

## Recovery Validation
After a recovery exercise, teams should validate service functionality and document lessons learned.
