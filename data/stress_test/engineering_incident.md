# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Engineering Incident

## Incident Ownership
The incident commander coordinates response activities during a major production incident. Service owners provide technical direction.

## Incident Communication
Major incidents require status updates through the approved incident communication channel until service is restored.

## Incident Severity
Engineering incident severity is based on customer impact, duration, and affected functionality.

## Incident Timeline
Responders should record significant events and decisions in the incident timeline during the response.

## Postmortem
Major incidents should have a blameless postmortem covering root cause, contributing factors, and corrective actions.
