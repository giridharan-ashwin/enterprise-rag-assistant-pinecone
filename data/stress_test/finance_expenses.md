# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Finance Expenses

## Expense Submission
Business expenses should be submitted through the expense management system within thirty days of the transaction.

## Expense Receipts
Receipts are required for reimbursable expenses above the company threshold. Digital receipts are accepted.

## Expense Approval
Expense reports are routed to the employee manager for approval. Higher-value expenses may require an additional finance review.

## Travel Expenses
Approved business travel expenses must be associated with the applicable project or cost center.

## Expense Corrections
Rejected expense reports should be corrected and resubmitted through the expense system rather than created as a separate claim.
