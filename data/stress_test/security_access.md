# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Security Access

## Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for company systems that contain protected information. Approved authentication methods are documented in the security portal.

## Credential Sharing
Employees must not share passwords, tokens, or authentication codes with other people. Shared credentials are prohibited even during urgent support situations.

## Privileged Access
Administrative access is granted only when business justification is documented. Privileged access is reviewed quarterly.

## Access Requests
Access requests must identify the requested system and business justification. Approvers must verify the employee role before granting access.

## Access Removal
Access to protected systems should be removed promptly when an employee changes roles or leaves the company.
