# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Data Retention

## Retention Schedule
Records should be retained according to documented business and regulatory requirements. Data should not be kept indefinitely without justification.

## Deletion Requests
Approved deletion requests should be tracked to completion and include evidence that required systems were processed.

## Backup Retention
Backup retention should align with approved recovery and regulatory requirements for the associated system.

## Archive Access
Archived data should have controlled access and documented retrieval procedures.

## Retention Review
Data owners should review retention schedules periodically when business or regulatory requirements change.
