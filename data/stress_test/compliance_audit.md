# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Compliance Audit

## Audit Scope
Audit scope should define systems, processes, period, and control objectives being assessed.

## Audit Evidence
Evidence provided for an audit should be complete, traceable, and attributable to the relevant control.

## Control Owners
Each key control should have an assigned control owner responsible for operation and evidence.

## Remediation Plans
Control findings should have documented remediation plans with owners and target dates.

## Audit Follow-up
Open findings should be tracked until closure and validated by the appropriate control or audit owner.
