# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Support Escalation

## Severity Levels
Support severity is based on customer impact, business criticality, and the scope of affected users.

## Priority Escalation
High-priority cases should be escalated to the appropriate technical queue when the initial support team cannot resolve them.

## Customer Updates
Customers should receive status updates at intervals appropriate to severity and agreed service targets.

## Escalation Ownership
The receiving technical team becomes responsible for the next troubleshooting step while the support owner coordinates customer communication.

## Case Closure
A support case should include the resolution summary and customer-impact notes before closure.
