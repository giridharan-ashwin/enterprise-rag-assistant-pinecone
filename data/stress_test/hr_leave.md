# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Hr Leave

## Planned Leave Notice
Employees should request planned time off at least five business days in advance when practical. Managers may approve exceptions for urgent circumstances.

## Leave Scheduling
Planned leave requests must be submitted through the company scheduling system. Managers review requests based on team coverage.

## Leave Coverage
Employees taking planned leave should document active work and identify a backup contact before their absence.

## Emergency Leave
Unexpected absences should be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible. The scheduling system should be updated when the employee returns.

## Leave Approval
Managers approve planned leave after considering business coverage and previously approved absences.
