# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Hr Benefits

## Health Plan Enrollment
Eligible employees can enroll in the company health plan during the annual enrollment window. New hires receive a separate initial enrollment period.

## Health Plan Changes
Outside the annual window, health plan elections may be changed only after an eligible life event.

## Flexible Spending
The company offers a flexible spending account for eligible employees. Contribution elections are made during annual enrollment.

## Wellness Benefit
Employees can claim an annual wellness reimbursement for qualifying activities. Receipts must be retained for reimbursement requests.

## Benefits Support
Benefits questions should be directed to the employee services team through the internal benefits portal.
