# Synthetic Enterprise Policy — Vendor Management

## Vendor Onboarding
New vendors should complete security and risk assessment steps before access to protected company systems is granted.

## Vendor Risk
Vendor risk is evaluated using service criticality, data access, concentration risk, and control maturity.

## Vendor Reviews
Critical vendors receive periodic performance and risk reviews. Review frequency depends on vendor criticality.

## Vendor Offboarding
Vendor access should be removed when the contract ends or when access is no longer required.

## Vendor Evidence
High-risk vendors may be required to provide security evidence such as assessments, certifications, or test results.
