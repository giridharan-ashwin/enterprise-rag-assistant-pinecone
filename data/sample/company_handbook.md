# Example Company Handbook

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Teams may establish additional collaboration days based on business needs.

## Paid Time Off
Employees should request planned time off through the company scheduling system at least five business days in advance when practical.

## Security
Company systems must use multi-factor authentication. Employees must not share credentials or place confidential company information into unapproved third-party tools.

## Expenses
Business expenses should be submitted through the expense management system within thirty days of the transaction.
